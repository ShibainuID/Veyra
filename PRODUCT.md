# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a farm manager responsible for hundreds of cattle. They investigate one animal at a time, understand its financial exposure, and authorize insurance actions: applications, premium payments, insurer notifications, and claims. They often work on a tablet in or near the barn, in daylight, between physical tasks.

Farm operators enroll animals, locate cattle, and investigate alerts, but they cannot approve financial actions. Veterinarians and animal-health officers add assessment notes and have no payment authority. Insurance assessors receive sandbox submissions.

## Product Purpose

VEYRA connects cattle identification, indicative valuation, health observations, and insurance workflows through one Digital Livestock Passport. Success means a manager can connect a specific cow, the observations behind it, and an insurance action in one reviewable workflow, with every step traceable to the same animal ID and an audit trail.

## Positioning

Every alert, valuation, policy, and payment resolves to one animal and the evidence behind it, and nothing financial happens without the manager's explicit approval.

## Brand Personality

Precise, accountable, calm. VEYRA should feel like an evidence system you would trust with money: every number shows its source, unit, date, and uncertainty. Outputs are labeled `live`, `prototype`, or `simulated`, and the interface never overstates what a model, camera, or provider has established. The voice is plain and factual. It reports observations, not diagnoses, and states missing evidence directly.

The interface is field-ready: high contrast, large touch targets, and strong, unambiguous state colors that stay readable on a tablet in bright light. It stays tasteful and restrained, not loud.

## Anti-references

- AI-showcase dashboards that hype model capability or hide uncertainty.
- Dense, small-type financial back-office screens that cannot be used on a tablet in the field.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before conclusion.** Show the source, method, time, and uncertainty next to every estimate, match, and amount.
2. **The human holds the pen.** Consequential actions show exactly what will execute, and only an explicit approval moves them.
3. **Uncertainty is a first-class state.** Unassigned, ambiguous, missing, and stale states get as much design care as success states.
4. **One animal, one thread.** Every screen links back to the passport and the audit trail.
5. **Legible in the field.** Contrast, size, and touch targets are chosen for daylight tablet use first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements were stated. The working default is WCAG 2.2 AA, plus field-use extras: body text contrast of at least 7:1, 44px minimum touch targets (48px for primary actions), state shown through text and icons as well as color, and reduced-motion alternatives for every animation.
